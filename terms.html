Regulatory & Trade Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Please read these commercial terms carefully. All stockists, distributors, and healthcare partners are bound by these operational protocols.

1. Commercial Eligibility

Trade relations are only established with entities holding a valid Drug License (Form 20, 21) and a registered GSTIN. Copies of these documents must be submitted to the Meritorious Pharmaceuticals administrative office before the first billing.

2. Quality Assurance & Sourcing

We certify that 100% of our products are manufactured at WHO-GMP certified facilities. Meritorious Pharmaceuticals acts as the Marketing & Distribution bridge. While we ensure high-quality sourcing, clinical efficacy is backed by the respective manufacturer's regulatory filings and COA (Certificate of Analysis).

3. Pricing & NPPA Compliance

Product prices (PTR/PTS/MRP) are subject to change without prior notice, following NPPA (National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority) guidelines or changes in raw material costs. Taxes (GST) will be charged extra as per government norms at the time of invoicing.

4. Payment Terms & Penalties

All payments are to be made via RTGS/NEFT/Cheque in favor of "Meritorious Pharmaceuticals". Credit periods are subject to prior agreement. Cheque bounce cases will attract a penalty of ₹500/- plus legal interest, and immediate suspension of the supply chain.

5. Logistics & Cold Chain

It is the sole responsibility of the stockist/distributor to maintain storage temperatures (as mentioned on product packaging) once the stock is delivered. Meritorious Pharmaceuticals is not liable for potency loss due to improper storage at the distributor's end.

6. Expiry & Breakage Policy

Expired stock must be reported at least 6 months prior to the date of expiry for smooth processing. Shortages or breakages during transit must be mentioned on the LR (Lorry Receipt) copy; otherwise, claims will not be entertained.

7. Ethical Promotion

Partners are required to promote Meritorious products using only authorized scientific literature provided by us. Any misrepresentation of clinical data or unethical trade practices will lead to immediate termination of the partnership.

8. Legal Jurisdiction

In case of any commercial or legal dispute, the courts at Gurugram (Haryana) shall have exclusive jurisdiction over all matters concerning Meritorious Pharmaceuticals.
